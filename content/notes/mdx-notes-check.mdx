title: Notes render in full, not as cards
draft: true
---

A note is short-form. It gets a permalink so it can be linked and can appear in
the feed, but the stream at `/notes` shows the whole thing — scrolling past a
grid of cards to read two sentences is what makes short-form writing feel
unfinished.

Notes take a looser schema than posts: the date is required, the title is
optional.
